# E3.1: within-atlas tile ordering and cluster partitioning

Status: completed (Modal, tag e31_cloud); dedup follow-up in_progress (tag e31_dedup).

## Hypothesis
Packing visually similar tiles adjacently reduces atlas bytes; cluster-pure chunked
atlases beat random chunks. Largest effects expected for codecs with local/windowed
adaptation (lossless), small for intra-only transform codecs.

## Setup
N=500 per class (emoji 72px, photos 224px); orders: filename baseline, random x3,
luma-sorted, mean-color-sorted, kmeans-clustered, greedy nearest-neighbor; partitions:
4 random chunks vs 4 kmeans chunks. Codecs in scope: jpeg:80, webp:80, png, webp_ll
(avif/jxl measured, out of scope since the 3-format decision). Modal 2-CPU container,
scripts e31_ordering.py + e31_cloud.py, commit at run time. ~30 min, <$0.40.

## Headline numbers (in scope, vs individual files at same settings)
- Lossy (jpeg, webp): ordering is worth at most ~1%; adaptation penalty not fixable by
  ordering. Cluster-chunking ~= random chunking.
- emoji PNG: filename (codepoint) order is the BEST order (-8.6% vs individual);
  similarity sorts recover most of the random-order penalty but do not beat semantic
  order; kmeans chunks beat random chunks by 3.2pp.
- photos PNG: sorted orders +16.6..+16.9% vs individual (baseline -1.4%);
  photos webp_ll: sorted +10.4..+11.5% (baseline -5.4%).

## Root cause of the big photo effect (found by data inspection)
The Picsum photo set contains 108/500 pixel-identical duplicates (seeded URLs map to a
finite pool). Similarity sorting places clones adjacently, inside the LZ window of PNG
(32 KB) and lossless WebP, which dedupes them; intra-only codecs (jpeg, lossy webp)
show ~0 effect, exactly matching the LZ signature. The gain is duplicate exploitation,
not generic similarity. Follow-up e31_dedup re-runs photos on the 392 unique tiles to
measure the clean ordering effect.

## Reframed finding (real and useful)
Collections with repeated tiles (product grids reuse thumbnails, placeholder art,
variant images) get large FREE deduplication from a sorted atlas under LZ-class
codecs; individual files cannot dedupe across files at all. Quantify against
duplicate rate in the optimizer's cost model.
