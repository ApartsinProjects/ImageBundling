Image Bundling Study

An empirical study of bundling many small web images (icons, product thumbnails, decorations) into a single atlas image, versus serving them individually: byte savings, load timing, decode cost, across codecs (PNG, JPEG, WebP, AVIF, JPEG XL) and protocols (HTTP/1.1, HTTP/2, HTTP/3).
