# Candidate venues (no APC, Q1/Q2)

Constraints: no article-processing charge (subscription/decline-OA route free, or
diamond OA), and Scimago SJR Q1 or Q2 in a relevant category. All six below are hybrid
subscription titles: the closed route costs the author nothing; the advertised APC
applies only if you opt into open access, which you decline.

| # | Journal (publisher) | Quartile / category | Free route | Scope fit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Computer Networks (Elsevier) | Q1, Computer Networks & Communications (2024) | Hybrid, free if you decline OA | HTTP/1.1/2/3 concurrency, tc-netem testbed, network timing |
| 2 | Software: Practice and Experience (Wiley) | Q2, Software (2024) | Hybrid, free if you decline OA | Whole-paper fit: applied systems measurement + released tool |
| 3 | Signal Processing: Image Communication (Elsevier) | Q2, Signal Processing (2024-25) | Hybrid, free if you decline OA | Matched-quality byte-savings sweep across JPEG/PNG/WebP |
| 4 | J. Visual Communication and Image Representation (Elsevier) | Q1 (2024-25) | Hybrid, free subscription route | Image representation/coding; less web-systems |
| 5 | IEEE Transactions on Multimedia | Q1 (2024) | Free traditional route (page-charge flag) | Multimedia systems + codecs |
| 6 | Multimedia Tools and Applications (Springer) | Q1 (2024) | Hybrid, free subscription route | Tool + multimedia; reputation caveat |

## Top 3
1. **Software: Practice and Experience (Wiley, Q2)** — best whole-paper fit: an applied
   measurement study plus a released optimizer tool is exactly its remit.
2. **Computer Networks (Elsevier, Q1)** — highest rank; direct home for the
   HTTP/1.1/2/3 + testbed contribution.
3. **Signal Processing: Image Communication (Elsevier, Q2)** — natural venue for the
   matched-quality codec byte-savings sweep.

## Excluded
- **ACM Transactions on the Web** — went fully open-access Jan 2026, ~$1,300 APC unless
  waiver/ACM-Open institution. Excluded on the no-APC constraint.
- **PeerJ CS** ($2,155 APC), **IEEE Access** (APC), **MDPI** Electronics/Applied
  Sciences (APC), **Journal of Web Engineering** (Q3).

## Flags to confirm before submitting
- **IEEE TMM**: free route but mandatory overlength page charges (~$220/page beyond 8
  published pages); a 10-15pp paper could incur ~$400-1,500. Not an APC, but a real cost.
- **Multimedia Tools and Applications**: 2023-24 retraction/special-issue problems;
  Q1 and free-route hold but it is reputationally the weakest of the six.
- Re-check the exact Scimago category/quartile line at submission time.

## Note on conferences
Quartiles apply to journals only. If strong CS conferences are acceptable (registration
fee, no APC; ranked by CORE A*/A not quartiles), the measurement-style fit is excellent:
ACM IMC, PAM, TheWebConf (WWW), ACM Multimedia. These often carry more CS prestige than
mid-tier journals.
